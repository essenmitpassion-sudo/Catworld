## Complete rules

5×5, beginning with one stone per color in opposite cells; the seed chooses this visible opening. Alternate placing one own stone in any empty cell. It pulls the nearest existing stone in each of the four orthogonal directions exactly one cell toward the new stone, irrespective of color. Adjacent stones cannot move and shield farther stones. Diagonal stones do not move. Resolve all four pulls simultaneously; colors never change and nothing is captured. Then check four or more contiguous own stones horizontally, vertically or diagonally. Either color can win on the other's action. Simultaneous lines are a draw. Full board without a line is a draw. Each move adds exactly one stone: at most 23 moves, no waiting rounds or cycles. All state public; deterministic seeded AI tie breaking. Seed affects the public opening and deterministic AI tie choices, never midgame dice.

One central action deliberately replaces the former three-action target: latest feedback prioritizes immediate comprehension and genuine redesign. Different tactical uses must emerge from placing, attracting your formation, and disrupting or accidentally completing the opponent's. Do not add a second input mode to satisfy an old count.

## Prior art checked

Known alignment and moving-stone games predate this project. Gigamic's Quixo uses extracting/reinserting edge cubes to push a whole row toward a five-in-line goal (https://en.gigamic.com/modern-classics/105-quixo.html; official rules https://export.gigamic.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/INS-RULES_QUIXO_03-2017.pdf). Momentum transfers a new marble's push through adjacent contiguous lines in eight directions, aiming to keep all one's marbles on board (publisher rules https://nestorgames.com/rulebooks/MOMENTUM_EN.pdf). SOG's public prototype combines distant nearest-only orthogonal attraction of either color with a four-in-line goal and monotonically filling board. This limited search does not establish novelty or legal priority; do not advertise a world first.
